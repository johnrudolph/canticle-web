## Introduction
[[Canticle]] is a game that takes place in the past and the present. You are stuck in a paradoxical, liminal world. Canticle is a cooperative, asymmetrical game.

One of you plays as the **Emperor** in the past, building a great city. You will choose between various paths and technologies. The city you build will determine what Relics are left behind in the future, the characteristics of the Golem who haunts the city, and the Prophesies that future Wanderers will fulfill to win the game.

The rest of you play as **Wanderers**, stuck between worlds, watching the past unravel a ruined world before your eyes. You explore and search the ruins of the great city, collecting Relics with powerful abilities. As you search you will find a variety of Prophesies, related to specific Relics and Locations. As you fulfill them, you gain 📜. This is how you win the game.

There are an extremely wide variety of branches and locations for the Emperor, and ways to earn 📜 for the Wanderer. Explore the world of Canticle deeply to find them all. 
## Components
#### Locations
Locations comprise the map of Canticle. The game begins with 6 random Locations present, and the Emperor will add a new Location to the map every round. Wanderers move between them and search them for Relics. Each Location has: 

- Name
- Art
- Lore: a bit of context that lets you know about the kinds of things you find there.
- Rule: a special new rule that permanently affects gameplay.
- Resource Production: what the Location produces for the city
- Resource Requirement: what resources must already be produced by other adjacent Locations in the city in order to construct this.
- Fixture outlines: these instruct the Emperor to place fixtures such as Monolith Shards.
- Relics: this tells you which type of relic to place there when you build this location, and how many of each.
#### Wanderer pawns
These represent the Wanderers. Each Wanderer has one.
#### Golem
The Golem haunts the city and attacks Wanderers.
#### Golem cards
This is the deck that the Golem draws from to take actions. 
#### Golem mat
The Golem's mat that contains spaces for golem cards and glyphs.
#### Glyphs
Glyphs are passive abilities that modify the Golem. The Golem adds glyphs that make it more dangerous, and wanderers and can add glyphs that make it less dangerous.
#### Relics
Wanderers discover Relics by searching Locations. Most Relics have actions and passive effects that help Wanderers fulfill prophesies in a wide variety of ways. Wanderers equip Relics by placing them on their Wanderer mat, use them during their turn, and can freely pick up and drop them on their location.
#### Wanderer Mat
Each Wanderer has a dedicated mat. The mat has spaces for their Relics, and a HP bar that represents their health.
#### Emperor Mat
This is the mat the emperor uses to track round, infrastructure, and prophesy.
#### Tracker Tokens

- 3 health trackers. 1 for each Wanderer mat.
- 1 Prophesy tracker token
- 1 Round tracker token
- 12 Shame Tokens
- 12 Scar tokens
#### Infrastructure
Throughout the game, the emperor will build infrastructure around the city.  These tokens begin on the emperor's mat, and get played on the board. The four types of infrastructure are defined in the Emperor phase section.

## Player count
Canticle is an asymmetrical game with an Emperor and Wanderers. The minimum number of Wanderers is 2. In a 2 player game, the players take turns performing the Emperor's turn. In a 3 or 4 player game, 1 player plays as the Emperor while the others play as Wanderers.

## Setup

1. Set the Golem Mat near the gameplay area. Shuffle all golem cards and form a face-up deck. draw the first 3 golem cards and lay them to the right of the deck. shuffle the glyph deck and set it nearby.
2. Give each Wanderer a Wanderer Mat. Place a Health Tracker at the highest number of your health tracker.
3. Set all relic decks, separated by type, facedown near the Emperor. Shuffle each.
4. The Emperor shuffles all locations, finds the first 6 starter locations, and places them in the configuration shown below. Shuffle the rest of the location cards to form the location deck near the emperor. The emperor draws 5 locations. For each location, draw the number of relics from the corresponding pile and place those relics facedown on the locations.
5. Place the Emperor's mat in front of the emperor and place all infrastructure tokens on their mat. 

4. The Emperor places the Golem on any space. Each Wanderer places their wanderer token on any space. 

# Gameplay
The game takes place over 8 rounds. Each round, the emperor, then the wanderers, then the golem will play. arrange your seats in a way where that will naturally happen in clockwise order.

In a 5 player game, one player plays as the Golem. On the Golem phase, they pick up all cards that the Golem will play, and plays them in any order they choose. When the Golem moves or attack, they decide where it moves and who it attacks. They win the game if the Wanderers and Emperor lose.
### Emperor phase

1. Play 1 Location from your hand. If it has “Must be built adjacent to,” it must be adjacent to Locations that produce those symbols. Read the Location aloud to the Wanderers.
2. If the Location has Relics, draw Relics from the matching Relic deck. If you come across Relics with backs that specify a Location, skip them unless you have drawn that Location. If the Location has the ⭐ symbol, there are specific relics from that deck you must include. Search the Relic deck as necessary to find the Relics with this Locations name on it.
3. Play 2 Infrastructure tokens if you have any in your pool (see infrastructure rules below).
4. Discard as many cards from your hand as you want.
5. Draw back up to 5. If your draw pile is empty, shuffle your discard pile.

designer notes on being the emperor
- it's most fun if you keep table talk to a minimum. Remember that you can see glimpses of what's happening in the future, but you don't get to talk strategy with them directly
- you are a game master. this role is built for you to deliver wanderers the most fun and interesting experience possible. So if you're playing with returning players, give them locations they haven't seen before. If you're playing with first timers, show them the locations you think are coolest. During the initial setup, you are highly encouraged to pick 6 starting locations that suit your wanderers best. 
#### Infrastructure tokens
Each turn you play 2 tokens on the mat. The rules of each are as follows: 

- Monolith Shard (12): There can only be 1 Shard per space. These have no intrinsic effect, but many magical Relics interact with Shards.
- Fruit tree (2): When Wanderers enter this space, they gain 1 action that they must use on this turn.
- Road (10): When Wanderers move into a space that contains a Road, they can continue into an adjacent space for free. A space that contains roads produces the symbols of all other spaces with roads that connect to that spaces. Example: Spaces A, B, and C are contiguous and all contain roads. Space C proudces 🐾, and so thus all 3 spaces produce 🐾.
- Walls (2): The Golem cannot enter spaces with Walls.  When placing Walls:  
	1. You cannot place it on the Golem’s space
	2. You cannot trap the Golem. The Golem must be able to reach all Locations that do not have Walls.

### Wanderer phase
Wanderers may always choose what order they take their turns in.
  
On your turn, you take actions. The number of moves and actions you have on your turn depends on the number of Wanderers:  
  
- 2 active Wanderers: 3 Actions
- 3 active Wanderers: 2 Actions

Actions available:  

1. 🦶: Move 1 space in any direction
2. 🔎: Search current location for a Relic
3. 🌿: increase Health by 2 (if you are at 0 HP this is the only action you can perform)
4. Perform the “Action” from one of your Relics equipped to the left.

Relic rules:  
  
- If you discover an Event, resolve immediately and trash.
- You may immediately equip Relics on this mat. It must go in a slot that matches its type (🖐️, 🧠, etc.). If you already have a Relic in that slot, you may drop it.
- You may pick up face-up Relics and drop Relics in your space at any time (even if it is not your turn). This does not cost an action. This means if an ally is on your space, you can give them a Relic on their turn at no cost.
- Dropping a relic leaves it face-up on your current space.
  
Using your basic 🦶 action, you move 1 space to any adjacent Location (Wanderers must always be on a Location). But many 🦵 Relics allow you to travel extra spaces when you move. If you move multiple spaces, you cannot stop midway to perform actions.

Other Symbols  

- 🌿 increases your health. Unless otherwise stated, you use 🌿effects on yourself.
- 🪡: Bound. Relics with this symbol must equip after discovering, unless this slot is Scarred, or already has a Bound Relic. You cannot drop, tuck, trash, or trade this.

Status Tokens  
  
Placed in a Relic slot of your choice. No Relic can go in that slot while the token remains.  

Shame has no intrinsic value, but interacts with many Relics and Locations.

### Golem phase
  
Resolve from right to left. The number you play is dictated by the current Round number, on the Emperor’s mat. Resolve each card immediately as it is drawn. If the card is tapped (face-down) do not resolve it, and discard face-up as with any other card. If the Draw Pile is empty, shuffle the Discard pile. If the card is a Glyph, it is added to the Glyph section below. If full, you must remove one.

Various cards will "tap" a queued Golem card. This means it is face-down. When you resolve a face-down Golem card, it has no effect.

Other rules:  

- If you ever draw an action the Golem is not able to take, ignore it. For example, if the card says to enter a space the Golem cannot enter.
- 🦶 should always take the shortest path to the nearest Wanderer. If multiple Wanderers are the same distance, the Emperor chooses. Ignore if already on a space with a Wanderer.
- 🌿 remove one Golem Modifier. The Emperor chooses, without any discussion.
- 👊 attacks Wanderers in the Golem’s space. Unless otherwise stated, it attacks 1 Wanderer. If there are multiple Wanderers who can be targeted, the Emperor chooses.
- If there is ever ambiguity about which Wanderer is targeted, the rules on the Golem cards and Modifiers always take precedence. If still ambiguous, the Emperor chooses.

# Game end
At the end of the 8th round, count your prophesy. If you reach 12 prophesy, you win. If you reach 16 prophesy you have an elite victory.

# Adversary mode
If you have 3 or more players, one of you may play as the Goelm, against the rest of the players. The game plays essentially the same, with a few key rules changes: 

1. On the Golem's turn, the adversary player picks up all the cards the Golem will play, and chooses which order they are played.
2. Any time the Golem moves or attacks they decide where it goes and whom it attacks.
3. If the Golem gives a Wanderer a Scar, the adversary decides which slot is scarred.

At the end of the game, the adversary wins if they Wanderers have not reached 12 prophecy.
